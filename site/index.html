For Katia Designs: a Meta agent that asks before it spends

Built Nov 3, 2026 by Frederic de Lavenne de Choulot

Approve a budget change, then change the budget yourself.

Before it sends anything, the agent reads the account again. If your approval no longer describes what it finds, it refuses and says why. Try it on a simulated ad set built around your Crystal Cross ad; it takes about a minute, or watch the same run.

Real: your public ads and catalog availability, each with source and time. Simulated: spend, orders, customers, targets, account structure. Executed code: rules, approvals, journal. Not shown: a connection to your ad account, or this agent deployed for another client; neither exists yet.

                How the project would run
                
      
                  
        
                1. Connections and reconciliation. Read-only access to Meta and Shopify; the week reconciled against both, with the method written down.
                  2. 
        
                2. Daily report and recommendations. Alerts and explained calls like the ones above, on your data and your targets.
                  3. 
        
                3. Supervised writes. Budget changes and pauses behind approvals, limits, the journal and the stop switch.
                  4. 
        
                4. Pilot. A few ad sets under watch; issues fixed before anything wider.
                  5. 
        
                5. Handover. Code in your repository, deployment notes, documentation.
                  6. 
      
                
      
                Depends on a Meta system user with access to the ad account, a Shopify custom app with order and customer read scopes, your targets (new-customer cost, cost per purchase, the daily increase limit), and where you want approvals to land.
